## THE MECHANISM (teach the real thing)

Here's what's happening, in plain English:

A slice is a **face-to-path problem.** At impact, your clubface is pointing
right of the direction your club is traveling. That difference is sidespin —
and sidespin is what turns a good-looking shot into a carve to the right.

Two things create it:

1. **An open face** — the clubface pointing right of your target at impact.
2. **An out-to-in path** — the club swinging left across the ball (that
   "over the top" move) to try to save the shot.

Fix the relationship between those two and the ball has no reason to curve.
That's it. Not more effort. Not a rebuilt swing. The right change, in the right
order, so you **get better from day one — no "get worse to get better."**

That's what the series walks you through.
