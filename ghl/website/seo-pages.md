# SEO Website Pages — GHL Website Builder

Duplicate this template for each page. Swap city, service, and coach name during onboarding.

**SEO checklist per page:**
- Title tag: `{Service} in {City} | {Coach Name}`
- Meta description: 150–160 chars with city + CTA
- H1: matches primary keyword
- Pricing section (transparency helps local SEO)
- CTA button → `/book` or intro funnel
- Internal link to 2+ other service pages
