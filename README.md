## Why GHL (not custom SaaS)

- Coaches never learn a new platform — they text you; the system runs in the background
- You control quality: one master snapshot, deployed consistently
- Rebilling margin on SMS, email, and AI usage
- White-label under your brand (`app.ejsgolf.com` or similar)
- Faster to market than building scheduling + CRM + ads from scratch
